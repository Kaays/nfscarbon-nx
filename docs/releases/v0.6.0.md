Need for Speed: Carbon (Xbox 360) running natively on the Nintendo Switch. This release adds the **French, Italian, German and Spanish discs**: seven editions in all, each with its own profile-guided NRO.

> **Needs your own copy of Need for Speed: Carbon for Xbox 360**, one of the editions below (a `.iso` or the extracted disc with `default.xex`). No game files are included here.
>
> **Needs an overclock.** The game is CPU-bound and does not run well at the Switch's stock clocks. Everything below was tested at **CPU 1683 / GPU 844 / RAM 2133 MHz** with the fourth CPU core.

## Install

Same as before ([README](https://github.com/Kaays/nfscarbon-nx#install-prebuilt-nro)): the NRO, `nfscarbon.toml` and your disc's files in `game_root/`, in one folder on the SD card, and a [Sphaira](https://github.com/ITotalJustice/sphaira) forwarder with **4 CPU cores** and a **39-bit address space** ([README, fourth core](https://github.com/Kaays/nfscarbon-nx#experimental-the-fourth-cpu-core)). One folder per edition, for example:

```text
sdmc:/switch/nfscarbon-nx-fr/    nfscarbon.nro   nfscarbon.toml   game_root/ (French disc)
sdmc:/switch/nfscarbon-nx-de/    nfscarbon.nro   nfscarbon.toml   game_root/ (German disc)
```

Updating from v0.5.0 (USA and the two Europe discs): replace the NRO, keep the toml, `game_root/` and your saves. Nothing changed for those three editions apart from the version number.

## What's new since v0.5.0

**Four new editions: France, Italy, Germany, Spain**
- Each runs its own disc, with everything v0.5.0 brought: the Options > Video settings, the car shadow and reflection fixes, motion blur, the audio and video fixes.
- The game's menus, subtitles and speech are in the disc's language. The Options > Video rows added by this port are in English.
- Speed is the same as the other editions (profile-guided builds): 28-30 FPS in races, about 18-22 FPS at the start of Race Wars while the whole pack is on screen.

**How they were made** (for those curious): the code of these discs is the same build of the game as USA, moved around a little. The function list and every hook were carried over by matching the code. Two things needed more care:
- The small "this-adjust" thunks (two instructions reached through a vtable) are packed in a different order on each disc. They are now all declared from the disc itself (`tools/add_thunks.py`): before that, the French disc could not start a race.
- Groups of identical small functions cannot be told apart by their code, so they were added by hand (the German disc skipped three of them).

## Known issues

- Rare crashes in police chases, seen in the Europe (Nordic) build. The same code is in the other builds; cause not found yet.
- In Neville's garage cutscene at the start of the career, Neville stays hidden with the muscle car (with the tuner only for a moment). Seen in all six European builds; USA not checked lately.
- A rare one-frame white flash in long races; once in a while a few black frames at a race start.
- The first race stutters while it compiles pipelines (only the first time).
- The Options > Video rows are in English in every edition.

## Reporting problems

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Kaays/nfscarbon-nx/issues) and attach `logs/nfscarbon_*.log`, `logs/rex/rex_perfil.log` and, for a crash, `logs/rex/rex_crash.log` from the game's folder, plus `unregistered_targets.txt` if there is one next to the NRO. Say which edition, which clocks, and whether the fourth core was enabled.
