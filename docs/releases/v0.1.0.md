# nfscarbon-nx v0.1.0 (preview)

First public build: a starting point for testing and for others to continue, not a finished port.

**Needs your own copy of Need for Speed: Carbon for Xbox 360, USA edition** (`.iso` or the extracted disc with
`default.xex`). No game files are included.

## Files

- `nfscarbon-nx.nro`: the port
- `nfscarbon.toml`: settings

Install: [README, Install](../../README.md#install-prebuilt-nro). Launch with title takeover (hold R while starting a
game) or a forwarder with a 39-bit address space.

## State

| Clocks (CPU / GPU / RAM) | Normal races | Race Wars | Sound in Race Wars |
|---|---|---|---|
| stock 1020 / 460 / 1331 | 16-19 FPS | 13-18 FPS | breaks up |
| 1785 / 921 / 2133 | 30 FPS in most places | 30 FPS in most places | fine |
| 1963 / 1152 / 2133 | steady 30 FPS | steady 30 FPS | fine |

Known issues: slow at stock clocks, sound breaks up in crowded races at stock clocks, intro videos slow at stock
clocks, a second car shadow, a short freeze at the start of races, the first race stutters while it compiles
pipelines.

## Feedback

Issues with the logs are the most useful thing you can send: `logs/nfscarbon_*.log`, `logs/rex/rex_perfil.log`, and
for a crash `logs/rex/rex_crash.log` plus the Atmosphère crash report. Say which clocks you used.
